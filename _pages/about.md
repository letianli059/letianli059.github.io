I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in Statistics and Biostatistics at Harvard Medical School (HMS) and the Harvard Pilgrim Health Care Institute (HPHCI), co-advised by [Prof. Rui Wang](https://www.populationmedicine.org/people/researchers/rui-wang) and [Prof. Wenbin Lu](https://statistics.sciences.ncsu.edu/people/wlu4/).

Before joining Harvard, I earned my Ph.D. from the Department of Statistics and Finance at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), supervised by [Prof. Zemin Zheng](https://bs.ustc.edu.cn/english/profile.php?id=302). I received my bachelor's degree in Statistics from the School of Mathematics at Northwest University.

## Research interests

- Reinforcement learning and multi-armed bandits
- Dynamic pricing and assortment optimization
- Dynamic treatment regimes and heterogeneous effects exploration
- Large language models (LLMs)
