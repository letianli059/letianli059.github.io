I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in Statistics and Biostatistics at the Harvard Pilgrim Health Care Institute (HPHCI) and Harvard Medical School (HMS), co-advised by [Prof. Rui Wang](https://www.populationmedicine.org/people/researchers/rui-wang) and [Prof. Wenbin Lu](https://statistics.sciences.ncsu.edu/people/wlu4/).

My current research interests include reinforcement learning, multi-armed bandits, dynamic pricing, optimal treatment regimes, and large language models (LLMs).

Before joining Harvard, I earned my Ph.D. from the Department of Statistics and Finance at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), where I was supervised by [Prof. Zemin Zheng](https://bs.ustc.edu.cn/english/profile.php?id=302). I completed my bachelor’s degree in Statistics at the School of Mathematics, Northwest University.
