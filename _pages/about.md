I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in Statistics and Biostatistics at the Harvard Pilgrim Health Care Institute (HPHCI) and Harvard Medical School (HMS), co-advised by Prof. Rui Wang and Prof. Wenbin Lu.

My current research interests include reinforcement learning, multi-armed bandits, dynamic pricing, optimal treatment regimes, and large language models (LLMs).

Before joining Harvard, I earned my Ph.D. from the Department of Statistics and Finance at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), where I was supervised by Prof. Zemin Zheng. I completed my bachelor’s degree in Statistics at the School of Mathematics, Northwest University.
